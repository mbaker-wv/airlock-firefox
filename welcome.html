Hatrack

A different hat for every account. Use several accounts on the same site, side by side, without them getting mixed up.

A few quick steps

  1. Password saving Checking…

    Hatrack turns this off for you, so Firefox can never fill in one account's password on another account's page.

  2. Turn off automatic work sign-in

    Firefox can sign you into your computer's work or school account by itself, in every tab, which defeats the point. Add-ons aren't allowed to change this, so please do it once: open Settings → Privacy & Security and uncheck “Allow Windows single sign-on for Microsoft, work, and school accounts”. If you don't see it, you're all set.

  3. Links from other apps optional

    When you click a link in your email or chat app, Firefox opens it in its shared, everyday session, signed in as whoever you used last. Turn this on and Hatrack moves those pages into a new hat instead. Firefox will ask to let Hatrack “access your data for all websites”: it only uses that to catch a page before it loads. It doesn't read or change the pages themselves.

    Using Multi-Account Containers' “Always open this site in…”? Leave this off, so the two add-ons don't both grab the same link.

  4. Remove Temporary Containers

    If you use Temporary Containers, it'll fight Hatrack over new tabs. Open Add-ons and themes (⇧⌘A) and remove it.

  5. Pin the Hatrack button

    Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the gear next to Hatrack, then Pin to Toolbar. The button shows which hat you're in: its number or initials, in its color.

How to use it

⌘T / Ctrl+TA new tab in a new hat: fresh and signed out
⌥⌘T / Ctrl+Alt+TA new tab in the same hat, already signed in
Right-click a link“Open Link in New Hat”, for a signed-out copy or a different account
Right-click a tab“New Tab in This Hat” or “New Tab in a New Hat”
The Hatrack buttonRename a hat, keep it signed in, or forget it

Each hat has its own color, shown under its tabs and in the address bar. When you close a hat's last tab, its logins and site data are erased (after the short wait you choose below), unless you ticked Keep.

Tip: sign in once, then press ⌥⌘T to open other pages for the same account. They share that hat's sign-in, so you won't be asked again.

Settings

The wait lets you reopen a tab you closed by accident (⇧⌘T) and still be signed in. Hats are always erased when Firefox restarts.

Privacy

Hatrack doesn't collect anything, doesn't track you, and never connects to the internet by itself. Everything it remembers stays in your Firefox.
